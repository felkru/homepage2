Music is often described as mathematics made audible. This project takes the geometric foundation of music theory and projects it into interactive 3D space, allowing you to visualize harmony, dissonance, and the mathematical structure of the scale.

[Visualize Musical Concepts](/musical_concepts.html)

## Explorations

- **Harmonic Series**: Visualize how overtones relate to the fundamental frequency.
- **Circle of Fifths**: See the geometric symmetry of western musical notation.
- **Frequency Relationships**: Explore the ratios that define the intervals we hear as "harmonious."

Built with Plotly.js, these visualizations provide a bridge between the art of music and the science of acoustics.
