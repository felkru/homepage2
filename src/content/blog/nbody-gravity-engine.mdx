Gravity is the architect of the cosmos. From spiral galaxies to binary star systems, the same simple force — every mass attracting every other mass — gives rise to endlessly complex structures. This simulation lets you play with that force directly.

## How It Works

Each particle exerts a gravitational pull on every other particle (an **N² direct summation**). When two particles get close enough, they **merge** — conserving momentum and mass — growing into larger bodies. Eventually, everything collapses into a single star, which then triggers a new cosmic cycle.

### Initialization Modes

- **Disk** — A rotating accretion disk, like a forming solar system
- **Binary** — Two colliding star clusters
- **Grid** — A uniform lattice that collapses under its own gravity
- **Chaos** — Random positions and velocities — pure entropy

### Controls

Press **H** to toggle the control panel. You can adjust:

| Parameter | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Particle Count** | Number of initial bodies |
| **Gravity (G)** | Strength of gravitational attraction |
| **Step Size (dt)** | Simulation speed |
| **Merge Threshold** | How close particles must be to merge |
| **Trail Persistence** | How long particle trails linger |

**Double-click** anywhere to spawn a massive singularity. **Click and drag** to attract particles toward your cursor.
