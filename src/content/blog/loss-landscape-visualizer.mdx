The training of a neural network is essentially a journey across a high-dimensional landscape. The "Loss Landscape" represents the error of the model relative to its weights. This interactive 3D visualizer lets you explore these terrains and see the paths taken by different optimization algorithms.

[Explore the Optimization Landscape](/optimizers.html)

## Concepts Explored

- **Gradient Descent**: The fundamental algorithm for finding the bottom of the valley.
- **Local Minima & Saddle Points**: Visualize the obstacles that can trap an optimizer.
- **Momentum & Learning Rates**: See how these hyper-parameters affect the trajectory of the optimization.

By mapping complex mathematical functions into a 3D space, we can build a better intuition for why some models converge efficiently while others struggle.
