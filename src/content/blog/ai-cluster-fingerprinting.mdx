As AI clusters grow in scale and importance, monitoring their usage and ensuring security becomes paramount. This project demonstrates how "Warden" scrubbing and fingerprinting can be used to verify AI software usage and identify specific workload signatures.

[View the AI Cluster Monitor Demo](/warden.html)

## Background

This visualization originated from research into verifying that AI systems are being used as intended. By analyzing the unique "fingerprints" left by different models and training runs, we can gain insights into cluster health, resource allocation, and policy compliance.

## Interactive Elements

- **Scrubbing Demo**: Watch how the Warden process filters and analyzes data streams in real-time.
- **Usage Verification**: See the process of matching current cluster activity against known model signatures.
- **Protocol Explorer**: A high-level view of the communication patterns within the AI stack.
